How it works

Set the edges.
Then start.

  1. 01

    Choose what stays open

    Add study sites and the apps you need. Google, ChatGPT, and Claude always remain available.

  2. 02

    Start a focus block

    Pick 60, 90, or 120 minutes—or use an open-ended session. Optional Focus and music controls activate with it.

  3. 03

    Come back without residue

    When the session ends, Study Block releases its timers and enforcement, restores redirected tabs, and records the session locally.
